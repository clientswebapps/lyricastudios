Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before ordering your custom song.

It’s easy and personal. You simply fill out a short questionnaire about the person, the occasion, your favorite memories, and any phrases or themes you’d like included. Our talented musicians and songwriters then turn your story into original lyrics and a custom melody. Your finished song will be delivered within 2 to 5 days, or as fast as 1 day with rush delivery.

We offer a wide variety of styles so your song feels just right for the moment. Choose from Pop, R&B, Country, Acoustic, Classical, Jazz, Gospel, Hip-Hop, and more. Each style can be tailored to match the personality of your loved one and the emotion you want to express.

Yes. Every order includes one free revision. If you’d like changes to the lyrics, tempo, or arrangement, just let us know and we’ll refine it until it feels perfect. Additional revisions are also available for a small fee if needed.

Standard delivery takes 2 to 5 business days. If you need it sooner, rush delivery is available and can be completed in as little as 1 day. You’ll also receive a preview first for approval before we finalize everything.

You’ll receive a high quality MP3 and WAV audio file, a beautifully designed PDF of your full lyrics, and a shareable link so you can listen online anytime. Everything is sent directly to your email and is yours to keep forever.
